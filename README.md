Aviation Risk Analysis
Project Overview
Our company is expanding into the aviation industry, and safety is the top priority. To guide our initial aircraft purchase decisions, I analyzed historical accident data from the National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) to determine which aircraft makes and models carry the lowest risk.

The Goal
The objective is simple: translate decades of accident records into actionable business recommendations. By comparing injury severity across different aircraft types, engine configurations, and flying conditions, this project identifies the safest options for our new business venture.

Repository Contents
Analysis: A Jupyter Notebook detailing the data cleaning, exploration, and visualization process.

Results: A presentation PDF summarizing key findings for business stakeholders.

Structure: This repo has been cleaned of default template files to ensure it is organized and portfolio-ready.
